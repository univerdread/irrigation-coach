# 3-minute demo script (draft)

Check the submission format first (video? live pitch? repo?). Everything shown is simulated and labelled; say so out loud once.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Title slide, photo of a smallholder pump | Farmers who just bought a pump can't see their root zone, so they water by habit, usually too much. In Mozambique, simple soil feedback halved water use. Our coach gives that feedback with the phone they already have. |
| 0:20–0:40 | Phone in flight mode: app opens | Flight mode on. Everything runs on the phone: soil map, crop-water maths, model, voice. |
| 0:40–1:05 | Plot tab: soil map → ribbon test → bucket test | Twenty minutes once, with the dealer. The open soil map says clay loam here, with its uncertainty; the ribbon test corrects it. A 20-litre bucket in 6 seconds tells us the pump. |
| 1:05–1:30 | Today: "Run the pump for 4 h 10 min" + Play | Then ten seconds a day: one sentence in Kiswahili. *Washa pampu kwa saa 4 na dakika 10.* |
| 1:30–1:50 | Next day → rain question → "No pumping needed today" | Most days the answer is: don't pump. That's where the fuel savings come from. If it doesn't know the rain, it asks. It never guesses a number. |
| 1:50–2:10 | Soil check disagrees → paused | When the photo check and the water count disagree, it shows no minutes and asks for a second check. Repeated disagreement goes to the extension officer. |
| 2:10–2:30 | Why & log: inputs with provenance, recompute | Every plan is logged with its inputs and where they came from, so anyone can recompute it by hand. With spot checks, that log becomes the evidence lenders and Kenya's results-based irrigation financing need. |
| 2:30–2:50 | 30-day chart: 225 m³ vs 500 m³ (simulated) | In a simulated month the coach uses 225 m³ instead of 500, with nothing draining past the roots. We claim fuel, fertilizer and labour savings for the farmer, not basin water savings. |
| 2:50–3:00 | Closing slide | Small AI: one decision, one plot, offline, in the farmer's language. |
